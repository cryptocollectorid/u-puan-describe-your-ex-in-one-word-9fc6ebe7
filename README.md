## The word

> Extinct

7 characters. The ex is extinct — gone the way of the dinosaurs, never coming back. The joke lands in the prefix itself: a real word that already starts with "ex" and means gone forever.

## Why this word

Built for the judge's brief, in order:

1. **One word, instantly a verdict on an ex** — "Extinct" reads as the final status of a former partner: done, gone, fossil record only.
2. **Comic punch** — the ex didn't just leave; the whole species died out.
3. **Surprise** — not the obvious "ghosted" or "blocked"; a nature-documentary verdict on a relationship.
4. **Craft** — exactly one word, spelled correctly, no punctuation tricks.
5. **Recognition** — everyone knows what extinct means, and everyone has an ex they'd happily file under it.
